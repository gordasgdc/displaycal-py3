# Instrument illustrations

Original vector artwork created for DisplayCAL-CG on 2026-10-19 (colorimeter,
spectro_puck, spectro_pen, other). They are generic drawings of an instrument
type resting on a screen; they are **not** photographs or copies of any
manufacturer's product images, logos or trade dress, and imply no certification
or partnership with any manufacturer. Licensed with the project (GPL-3.0-or-later).

The grey square marks the measurement spot only. During real measurements the
patch window is a separate, neutral window; nothing in these drawings is
rendered over patches.
